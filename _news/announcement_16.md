Prof. Kaur was invited for a department seminar at the Physics and Astronomy department of Trent University. She gave a talk on "Next-Generation Ion Conducting Membranes for Clean Energy Systems using Two-Dimensional Materials" where she discussed her recently published work on anion exchange membranes and showed some of the current work of her graduate students.
