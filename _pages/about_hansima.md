### Biography

Hansima completed B.Sc. in Physics from University of Jaffna, Sri Lanka in 2019. She joined the Kaur group to pursue M.Sc. in Physics in Winter 2023 and completed in Spring 2025. Her research focused on synthesis and characterization of titanium carbide MXenes and fabrication of solid-state electrolytes using novel MXenes and polymer composites. In leisure time, she enjoys singing, drawing, and cooking.
