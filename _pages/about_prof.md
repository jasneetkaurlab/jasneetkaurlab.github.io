Dr. Jasneet Kaur is an Associate Professor in the Department of Physics and is cross-appointed to the Yousef Haj-Ahmad Department of Engineering at Brock University. Her research focuses on the synthesis, surface engineering, and advanced characterization of two-dimensional (2D) materials and nanostructured materials for clean energy storage and conversion, electrocatalysis, and sustainable technologies.

Before joining Brock University in 2021, Dr. Kaur was a Mitacs Elevate Postdoctoral Researcher at Toronto Metropolitan University, where she developed 2D materials for energy conversion and storage while collaborating with Canadian and international research and industrial partners. She previously completed a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Naples, Italy, conducting interdisciplinary research on graphene and other 2D materials. Dr. Kaur earned her Ph.D. in Experimental Physics from Eternal University, India, following B.Sc. (Honours) and M.Sc. degrees in Physics from the University of Delhi.
